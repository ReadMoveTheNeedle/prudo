Who we are

Prudo is made by ACCOD INC., a company based at 60 rue des Pinsons, Gatineau (Québec) J9A 2V6, Canada. The person in charge of the protection of personal information at ACCOD INC. can be reached at movetheneedleteam@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist on this website, we collect your email address, the date you joined, and the page or link you came from (for example, our newsletter). Nothing else. This website does not use cookies, analytics or ads.

Like any website, the services that deliver this page (GitHub and Cloudflare for hosting, Google for fonts, Supabase for the waitlist) automatically receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type, to show you the page and keep it secure. We don't use this information ourselves.

Why we use it

  • To tell you when Prudo is ready and give you early access.
  • To send you a few emails about Prudo's launch.

We never sell your email, and we don't use it for advertising.

Where it's stored

Your email is stored with our database provider, Supabase, Inc. Its servers may be outside Quebec, including in the United States, where different privacy laws apply. Only the founders of Prudo can access the list.

How long we keep it

Until 6 months after Prudo launches, or until you ask us to remove it, whichever comes first. Then we delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your email, or withdraw your consent, at any time, by writing to movetheneedleteam@gmail.com. Every email we send also lets you unsubscribe. We answer within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can file a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.
