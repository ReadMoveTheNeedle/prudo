Our Story

From newsletter to app

Prudo comes from the team behind Move The Needle (readmovetheneedle). A newsletter where we provide the strategies you need to maximize your productivity and build a life you respect.

We focus on productivity systems, implementing habits, staying focused, and avoiding procrastination, all personally tested and science-backed. But we knew that written systems could only get people so far.

So we tried out different apps: Habit trackers, time-blockers, and calendars. And while all of them got the job done, they didn't work with you. They expected you to flawlessly show up and know how to engineer the perfect system.

That's how we came up with Prudo. A personalized productivity buddy that doesn't just give you the tools for your goal. He also helps you get better, see your progress, and keep your goal in one place.

Everything we learned about productivity went into building an app that actually makes those systems applicable.
